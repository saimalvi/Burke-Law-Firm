What's at Stake

An OUI Conviction Can Change Everything

A Massachusetts OUI is more than a traffic ticket. The consequences can follow you for years — and they begin the moment you're charged. Here's what you could be facing.

License Suspension

You can lose your driver's license — and your ability to get to work — for months or years.

Fines & Fees

Court fines, assessments, and program costs can total thousands of dollars on a first offense alone.

Possible Jail Time

Even a first offense carries the possibility of incarceration. Repeat offenses carry mandatory minimums.

Ignition Interlock

You may be required to install — and pay for — an ignition interlock device on your vehicle.

Insurance Increases

An OUI can raise your auto insurance premiums dramatically — often for many years.

Criminal Record

A conviction creates a permanent record that can affect jobs, housing, and professional licenses.

How We Fight Back

Our OUI Defense Strategies

An OUI charge is not a conviction. There are many ways to challenge the prosecution's case — and we examine every one.

Challenging the Stop

Police need a valid reason to pull you over. If the stop was unlawful, the evidence may be thrown out.

Field Sobriety Tests

These tests are subjective and affected by nerves, footwear, medical issues, and road conditions.

Breathalyzer Errors

Breath-test machines must be properly calibrated and operated. Errors and maintenance gaps can invalidate results.

Officer Credibility

We scrutinize the police report, body-cam footage, and testimony for inconsistencies and procedural mistakes.

Massachusetts Penalties

OUI Penalties by Offense Level

Massachusetts OUI penalties escalate sharply with each offense. The figures below are general ranges under M.G.L. c. 90, § 24 — your actual exposure depends on the specific facts of your case.

Offense Potential Jail Fines License Loss
1st Offense Up to 2½ years (often probation with an alcohol education program) $500 – $5,000 Up to 1 year (hardship license possible)
2nd Offense 60 days – 2½ years (mandatory minimum applies) $600 – $10,000 2 years; ignition interlock required
3rd Offense 180 days – 5 years (felony; mandatory minimum applies) $1,000 – $15,000 8 years; ignition interlock required
