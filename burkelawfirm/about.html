Our Story

By Your Side When You Need Us Most

Burke Law Firm was founded on a simple belief: that ordinary people deserve the same caliber of legal representation as the insurance companies and prosecutors they're up against. For years, we've stood beside Massachusetts families during some of the hardest moments of their lives — after a serious injury, or when facing an OUI charge that threatens everything they've worked for.

We are a plaintiff-side personal injury and criminal defense firm. That means we represent the injured and the accused, never the insurers or the state. Every case gets the personal attention of an attorney — not a case number passed between paralegals. When you call us, you reach a real person, day or night.
